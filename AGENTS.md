# Agent instructions

Commit messages must list only the human author; never add `Co-Authored-By` trailers or AI-agent attribution.

After finishing a change (a PR or any other work), take a quick look at the landing website in `site/` (mainly `site/src/pages/index.astro`, plus `alternatives.astro` and `privacy.astro`) and update anything the change made outdated: features that were added, renamed, or removed, settings, permissions, screenshots, and wording.
